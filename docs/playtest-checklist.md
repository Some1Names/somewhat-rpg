# Playtest checklist: the whole game, in play order

One run-through that replaces opening the 18 per-system checklists in `specs/` (they keep the detail). Part A is solo, in the order a player meets things: sitting 1 = A1 to A9, sitting 2 = A10 to A21. Part B needs two or three players. Tick a line only after you saw it; on a failure write its number (A7, B3...) and what happened.
- **Synced 2026-11-10:** every script in the repo is in Studio (phase 0 of `ROADMAP.md`; the `studio` tag marks that sync). If the repo has moved past the tag since, sync again before playing.
- **(live)** = needs a live server (Studio can't save). **(needs the model in Studio)** = depends on something that exists only in Studio. A step with no admin helper named has none.
- **Admin panel** (`` ` ``; everyone is admin in Studio): PLAYER = XP, Yen, Level, Stat points, Materials +50 each, Learn all recipes, Finish prints, Give gear, Clear gear, each stat, Refund stat points; POWERS = GIVE SET, `<Set> mastery`, Overcharge, Reset cooldowns; STATUS = effects, Heal, Respawn; WORLD = raid bosses, TIME OF DAY.
- **Default keys:** Skill 1-5 = Z X C V T, Passive ability G, Dash Q, Block F, Reload R, Passive 2 (Photoshop's gallery) Y, Hide HUD F1, Item wheel LeftCtrl; B Backpack, J Quests, M Map, P Settings. The old checklists say E for block, F for the fifth skill and T for the gallery: out of date.
- **Test switches still on:** `SkillSetConfig.OwnAllSets` (every power owned: turn it off for A7), `TalismanConfig.OwnAll`, every fast-travel point unlocked. Block is on and basekit in the repo and in Studio (`GuardConfig.Enabled = true`, anyone can block), so A6 has its steps.

- [ ] Join and watch the output (no errors): a loading screen shows, then fades once the title is ready; the title reads SOMEWHAT, VERSION n and PLAY / UPDATES / HANDBOOK / SETTINGS over a camera drifting through `workspace.MenuCameras` (needs the model in Studio), or circling the spawn without it.

## A6. Melee, dodge, stamina and block
- [ ] Left click bare-handed: a 4-hit punch combo; each swing costs stamina (the last more) and halves your walk speed while it lasts; the stamina row drops and refills; hits show damage numbers and sparks.
- [ ] (at a Henchman, with A9) Let it swing: jabs show a white warning and a melee hit staggers them; the heavy shows red, lunges and can't be staggered; press Q in its wind-up and you take no damage; two dashes less than about 0.35 s apart: the second doesn't start; Admin PLAYER: Agility Set widens the window.
- [ ] Hold F to block (bare-handed and with a set held: anyone can): you walk at half speed and can't sprint, punch, shoot or cast (the skill bar refuses); let go and everything works at once; Q while blocking drops the block and dashes; typing in chat never blocks.
- [ ] Take a hit from the front while blocking: a melee hit does about 40% of normal damage, a shot about 50%, a skill about 70%, and a white spark shows; from behind or past 90° it is full damage and no spark; ultimates, grabs (Spine Lash, Black Hole, Tornado) and Burnt/Bleeding ticks go through at full damage and add no posture.
- [ ] Keep blocking until the white posture bar (it replaces the stamina row) is full: guard break, the block drops, the bar flashes red, you can't act for about 1 s or block for 2 s more, then posture is 0; it drains by itself (about 20 a second after 1.5 s unhit, about 5 while you hold unhit).
- [ ] Die, switch tools, unequip or Alt-tab while blocking: the next life or return has no stuck block or half speed and the stamina row is back.
